Rules of thumb

  • Never color alone — icon + words, so it works for color-blind users and in grayscale.
  • Say how to fix it — "Enter an email like name@company.com", not "Invalid input".
  • Errors stay, successes fade — errors persist until fixed; success toasts may auto-dismiss after 4–5 s.
  • Severity order — error, warning, success, info. One banner per region.
  • Not decoration — status colors are never brand accents or chart series; Ignis red is never decorative.

Grays — text tones and shades

Body text is never pure black. Pick the tone by how much the text should stand out — three tones per view is plenty.
